# 2025-2026-DEP-State-of-the-Community-Survey-Results
## HTML, Python, Excel, Tableau(to follow)

This [report](link) contains the results of the online survey conducted among members of the Data Engineering Pilipinas Facebook group.

## Objective:
To get continued understanding of the community composition and needs, and compare with last year’s survey.   

## Action From Results:
Results will continue to help our DEP community answer common questions on job opportunities, skills, salaries related to data engineering and other data-related careers.  These will also guide future programs and initiatives. Findings will be published in a public site (e.g. github.io, Tableau Public). Data will be anonymized using Bayesian network model methods, prior to release through github. 

## Background:
Data Engineering Pilipinas has now grown from 28000 members to 40000 members on Facebook. Spearheaded by Myk Ogbinar, it has grown to be a vibrant, engaged community with a lot of helpful programs for aspiring Data Engineers and other data-related career seekers.  The first survey showed answers to common questions like: What is our community’s salary profile? What skills are most relevant by data role? Is it necessary to get a master’s degree to get a high salary?  And many more. 
This year, we added new concerns on AI, shift schedule, job satisfaction, and online activities attendance.
